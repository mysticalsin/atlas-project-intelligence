ATLAS: reference sheet template, low-latency opening, patient turn-taking

Reference sheet (Amaris/Mantu template)
- Four-step wizard: Opp ID first, sources (upload via Workers AI toMarkdown,
  .pptx text and images read in the browser), grouped challenge questions,
  validation with key visuals and country/entity, then generate
- Word output: title, Executive Summary, one 8 x 2 table with a 3 cm label
  column, captioned visuals, Calibri throughout, template file name
- Never invents content ("To be completed"); client details scrubbed before
  any model

Opening, tone and latency
- Cached greeting that says who ATLAS is and why she is there, protected
  from noise; Opp ID asked first; natural reactions and a CHALLENGE section
  in the interviewer policy
- Prompt-cache friendly message order, session affinity, PCM cache,
  first-clause flush, semantic end of turn (smart-turn-v2); transcription
  runs once per answer (no speculative transcripts)

Patient turn-taking ("she answers too quick")
- Turn model asked at 0.7 s and trusted only when confident (p >= 0.85);
  1.2 s of silence when unsure; up to 2.5 s when it hears an unfinished
  thought, then at most a gentle nudge
- Never talks over the consultant; a consultant who carries on gets her
  started reply withdrawn (screen and history) and one merged answer
- Short sounds (clicks, breaths, a lone "No.") handled without loss or
  delay; echo judged on the microphone stream against playback reports
- Uptake acknowledgements ("Right.", "I see.") instead of continuers
- Per-language unfinished-sentence detection; numbers-only reply_gap and
  turn metrics for tuning
- Six adversarial review rounds; every regression test fails on the
  pre-fix code
