## Decisions (spec silent or not literally achievable), for review
- **Uncued city names.** An uncued city ("Lisbon queue") is neither pinned nor listed in `placesNotMapped`. ATLAS has no city gazetteer (geo.js never guesses from cities), and adding one is out of scope. `placesNotMapped` holds uncued country words ("Chad", "Jordan"), aliases and abbreviations ("Holland", "UK", "Mexico City") and ambiguous names ("Georgia" in free text, "Congo"). A cued mention of a country wins over an uncued one in the same interview.
- **Leading "the".** "the" before a name is not part of the name, so "in the Netherlands" pins NLD.
- **Cued lists.** Under the spec's list rule, "in Portugal and Jordan agreed" pins JOR. This is a documented limit; the pin text says "not necessarily the project location".
- **Word units.** A word unit must end at a word boundary: "3 percentage points" and "5 daily" give no chip, rather than showing "3 percent" or "5 day", a unit that was not said. Ranges only take a bare number *before* a span ("3 hours to 12 teams" stays "3 hours"). "Adjacent" means touching or separated only by whitespace.
- **No timePlan.** When `ready` has no timePlan (an older server), no phase is invented in full mode; gap mode still shows "focused".
- **Analysing.** "analysing…" clears on the next quality event or on `ended`. A `quality_warning` does not clear it, because echo and speech-to-text warnings are not analyst results.
- **Ledger flag wording.** The ledger flag "No verified measurement: …" is shown as "No measurement recorded: …", so no board element ever says verified, confirmed or approved (F5.3 #4).
- **referenceId.** `#gapSend` sends the reference id of the *interviewed* project: the project whose `gapNotes.requestId` matches, else the project that was current when `notes` arrived, else `ATLAS_APP.project()`. `#gapApply` passes that project's id. In the normal flow this equals `ATLAS_APP.project()?.library?.id`, but it stays right if the user switches projects before clicking.
- **Card class.** The cards use `.board-card`, not `.context-card`, because `tests/sculpture_browser.py` counts exactly 5 `.context-card` elements. The look is the same.
- **Separate proof card.** The proof answers are a separate card (`#gapPanel`), so they show even when the live ledger is off.
