## What was built
- **F3 claims (`claims.js`, words `claims`, `confirmations`; Access mode only).**
  - §2.4 DDL, with the unique `(ref_id, sentence_hash)` index and memoised `ensure`.
  - Candidates come from `splitSentences(raw answers[1..4])`, at most 30. A proposal must equal `split[idx]` at the current revision (else 409); at most 8 per call; re-proposing a sentence returns the existing row.
  - Confirmer: staffing (projectId + `refs.owner`) first, else the declared N+1, else 400. Independence: never the owner, the requester or a member (409).
  - Client kind: 503 BLOCKED_EXTERNAL (with its 4 needs) unless `CLIENT_CONFIRM=1` and the email is in `EXTERNAL_EMAILS`.
  - Each request expires after 14 days. Its draft names the consultant and N only; the body is only `<ORIGIN>/confirm#<id>`. It queues `teams` and `email_draft` outreach rows as `pending_approval`.
  - Inbox: filtered by the verified mail, open, unexpired, every claim current. The text is raw only with full access; otherwise `recordText(clientScrub(sentence))`, and the title is masked the same way.
  - Decisions: confirm, correct (note required, ≤ 500) or can't say. Wrong person 403, stale 409, expired 410. One audit row per claim: `claim <decision>`, `rev N claim <id> (kind, source)`.
  - Cancel: creator, reviewer or admin. The external role reaches only the inbox and the decision.
  - Currency: `'sentence'` (default) and `'revision'`, both tested. `refs.state.validation` and `status` are never touched.
  - Exports: `claimSummary`, `confirmationStatus` (current claims only, keyed by `claimKey`), `expire`, `CURRENCY`.
- **`/confirm` page (`confirm-page.js`).**
  - Self-contained: no external URL, no `<script src>`, no font, no animation. It works under the standard CSP and writes with `textContent` only.
  - Elements: `#confirmInbox`, `[data-decision]`, `#confirmSubmit` (enabled only when every point is decided), `#confirmDone`.
  - The hash focuses the request it names; an unknown id says the request is no longer open.
  - Dark Mantu palette (#101011, #3E007A, #F6EB4B); 44 px targets; no overflow at 320 px.
- **F4 outreach (`outreach.js`, words `outreach`, `staffing`; system word `teams-event`).**
  - Trigger: the invitation is unchanged. With `managerEmail` it also upserts staffing, and it queues exactly 2 pending rows (a repeat call adds none).
  - Quote-aware `parseCsv` (quotes, CRLF, CR, BOM, line numbers).
  - `POST /staffing/import`: the exact six-column header. Any other column (so any client, customer or account column) is dropped right after parsing and reported in `ignoredColumns`. More than 2,000 rows or 200,000 characters gives 413. Rejected rows carry line numbers. Due rows get a `project_end` invitation (source `staffing-csv`) plus outreach rows.
  - `GET /staffing`, `GET /outreach`. `POST /outreach/:id`:
    - `email_draft` becomes `drafted`, with `{to, subject, body, ics?}`;
    - `teams` becomes `blocked_external` with the exact reason, or `sent` / `failed` through `services.teams`; `MessageWritesBlocked` stops the conversation;
    - `phone` is always `blocked_external`; `dismiss` gives `dismissed`.
  - `daily`: both expiries always run; then due staffing rows and the `teams_seen` prune (2 days). It never sends.
  - `teams-event`: dedupe in `teams_seen`; install, uninstall, stop; snooze and decline act only for the invitee (same object id and tenant, which must also have a conversation), and anyone else gets "This card belongs to someone else."
- **Teams (`teams.js`, `teams-routes.js`): BUILT, NOT CONNECTED.**
  - `verifyBotActivity` is pure and makes the 9 checks. Keys come from the OpenID metadata and JWKS, cached 24 h, with any refetch at most once per 5 minutes (a failure included). Issuer: `https://api.botframework.com` only.
  - `teamsStatus`, `teamsClient` (tenant token cached until exp − 300 s; retry 429/412/502/504 at most twice; service-URL allow-list before any request; member lookup for routing only), `interviewCard`, `messageCard`.
  - `teamsRoutes`: 503 BLOCKED_EXTERNAL unless `TEAMS_ENABLED=1` and the 3 ids are set. Events are forwarded to `teams-event` (role system, user teams-bot), and an invoke is answered in the HTTP 200 body. Every response carries its own security headers.

## Decisions beyond the spec text (for the integration review)
- **`daily` returns only non-zero counts** (`gapsExpired`, `claimsExpired`, `staffingInvited`, `outreachQueued`, `teamsSeenPruned`). A quiet day's cron response stays `{invited: 0}`, and no key can overwrite `invited`.
- **Staffing due window.** An end date must be in [today − 30 days, today + 7 days] (the spec says only ≤ today + 7). Without the lower bound, importing a historical CSV would invite every consultant of every past project. There is no second `project_end` invitation for the same (email, project), whatever the first one's status, so a dismissal is never undone.
- **One submit decides every point** of a request (400 otherwise). This makes "done when every claim is decided" atomic, and the page's submit already requires it.
- **Additive fields:**
  - `ClaimRow.kind` and `ClaimRow.source` for decided claims (the badge needs the source), derived from the deciding request, so the §2.4 DDL is unchanged;
  - `draft.mailto`;
  - optional `subject` on a `queue` item;
  - request `status` shown as `stale` or `expired` for open requests (computed).
- **`claimSummary`** returns `{confirmed: current confirmed claims, total: every claim of the reference (stale included), current: current claims}`. `confirmationStatus` maps every current claim to its status, including `unverified`, `corrected` and `cant_say`.
- **Test seams:**
  - `verifyBotActivity(…, {now})`, `teamsClient(env, fetcher, {now, sleep})`, `teamsRoutes(…, {fetcher, teamsJs})`;
  - `ctx.currency`, which only tests set;
  - `resetBotKeyCache()` and extra exports (`messageCard`, `TAB_CSP`, `eventOf`, `REASONS`, `BOT_ISSUER`, `OPENID_URL`, `SERVICE_HOSTS`).
- **Outreach approval:**
  - a row whose subject is no longer open becomes `dismissed` ("The invitation or request is no longer open.") and is never sent;
  - `blocked_external` and `failed` rows can be approved again, for when Teams is connected;
  - an in-memory lock prevents a double Teams send;
  - a stopped conversation gives its own reason ("This person asked ATLAS to stop Teams messages.").
- **Bot tokens:**
  - the service-URL claim is read as `serviceurl` (the Bot Framework SDK constant) or `serviceUrl`;
  - a failed key fetch gives 503 (not 401);
  - `TEAMS_SERVICE_HOSTS` (optional, comma-separated) extends the allow-list ("extendable by config").
- **TeamsJS CDN fetch.** The spec's tab CSP blocks the CDN fetch of valid domains, and TeamsJS falls back to its bundled list. The CSP is kept as the spec states; widening it with `connect-src https://res.cdn.office.net` would be a Tony decision.
- **Manifest placeholders** are `TEAMS_APP_ID`, `TEAMS_BOT_APP_ID`, `ATLAS_HOST`, `TEAMS_DEVELOPER_NAME`, `TEAMS_DEVELOPER_WEBSITE_URL`, `TEAMS_PRIVACY_URL` and `TEAMS_TERMS_URL`. `accentColor` is `#3E007A`, the app's own purple.
