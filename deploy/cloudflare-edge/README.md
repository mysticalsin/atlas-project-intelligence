# ATLAS on Cloudflare's edge (test deployment)
A live, protected ATLAS at **https://atlas.tony-walteur.workers.dev**, with a real backend and nothing to host yourself.

```
Browser (the real ATLAS page, orb, worklet)
  │  Cloudflare Access: email one-time PIN; policy allows tony.walteur@gmail.com only
  ▼
Worker (src/app.js)  verifies the Access JWT (RS256, aud, iss, exp) → identity; host/origin/CSRF checks; strict CSP
  │ WebSocket /api/voice
  ▼
Durable Object "Interview" (one per signed-in person) → Session (src/session.js), same browser protocol as the Python gateway
  mic PCM → EnergyTurns → Workers AI nova-3 → llama-3.3-70b-fast interviewer (streamed, sentence-by-sentence aura-1 voice)
                                        └→ gpt-oss-120b analyst → InterviewLedger (exact quotes, next probe) → quality events
```
- **One source of truth:** `build_edge.py` generates `src/policy.generated.js` from `atlas_voice/*.py`. `test/parity.test.js` replays Python ledger scenarios and requires identical JS results, and `test/speech.test.js` does the same for turn detection. `tests/test_edge.py` fails if either is stale.
- **No provider keys:** Workers AI is reached through the `AI` binding. The only secret is `CSRF_SECRET`, generated by `deploy.sh`.
- **Scope:** voice interview, evidence ledger and notes. Wiki, Hindsight, JEV and Entra are **not** part of this edge deployment (the UI hides them). Nothing is stored server-side after a session. Test / synthetic data only on this personal account.

## Turn-taking (when ATLAS decides the consultant has finished)
An interviewer lets people think. The rules live in `src/session.js` (constants at the top) and are covered by `test/session.test.js`.
- **End of turn:** at a 0.7 s pause the turn model (`smart-turn-v2`) is asked once. Confident "finished" (p ≥ 0.85) ends the turn; unsure or no answer ends it after 1.2 s of silence; "unfinished" gives the consultant up to 2.5 s to go on. Very short answers skip the model and use the 1.2 s rule.
- **Never over the consultant:** ATLAS's audio waits while they are speaking (a click or a breath, under 0.15 s of voice, is closed after 0.45 s of quiet, longer than a comma pause; it is kept for 1 s and joins whatever they say next, or is transcribed when ATLAS is idle, so a lone "No." is still answered) or their last words are still being transcribed (at most 15 s: a transcription that never returns counts as no words). Her text appears on screen only as its audio starts.
- **They were not finished:** if they speak again while their last words are still being transcribed, or before they have heard 1.2 s of her actual reply (the "Right." does not count), the reply is withdrawn (removed from the screen with `agent_retract` and from the conversation history, not stored as an interruption) and both parts become one answer. A reply cut off by a genuinely new turn is recorded with what was heard, before that turn. A noise only delays the reply; a failed transcription counts as no words, so nothing is left unanswered.
- **A thought left hanging** (the model heard it unfinished, or the words trail off in the language heard: "and…", "um", "euh", "parce que", "weil"…; `unfinished()` in `src/languages.js`, never a question or a name) waits 2.5 s from the last sound, then gets at most a gentle nudge ("Take your time."), never a new question.
- **Echo:** only speech that starts while her audio is still playing in the browser (plus a 0.2 s tail) and matches the reply she was playing is treated as her own voice. Timed on the microphone stream itself (the browser's playback reports arrive in order with the microphone audio, so network delays cannot fool it); browsers without reports use 0.5 s after the last audio sent. An answer that reuses the words of her question afterwards is the consultant's. A short sound that may have been her echo is never mixed into their words.
- **Measured:** `reply_gap` (last consultant sound → ATLAS's first sound, ms), `turn_check`, `turn_early`, `turn_resumed`, `reply_withdrawn`, `turn_merged`, `still_thinking` are logged as numbers only. Tune `EARLY_PAUSE`, `TURN_PROBABILITY`, `END_SILENCE` and `THINKING_PAUSE` from them after real calls.
- **Acknowledgements** ("Right.", "I see.") only after a real answer (12+ words, not a question), never two turns in a row; continuers such as "Mm-hm" are not used because they mean "keep going".
- **Admin mode** keeps the same end-of-turn rules and the same patience on words that trail off, but no nudges and no acknowledgements.

## Deploy
```sh
npx wrangler login                      # one time, in Terminal (browser approval)
bash deploy/cloudflare-edge/deploy.sh   # runs the edge tests, deploys, sets CSRF_SECRET on first run
```
